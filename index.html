CAMPUS AMENITIES & SPECIALIZED SERVICES

Holistic Facilities & Sacred Sanctuary

An integrated self-sustaining eco-campus fostering healing, spiritual exploration, and community care.

Medical Sanctuary

1. MATRU SADAN HOSPITAL

Comprehensive Ayurvedic hospital and medical care center offering clinical diagnostics, Vaidya consultations, inpatient restorative wards, and integrative healing therapies.

Classical Pharmacy

2. RASSHALA

Authentic on-campus Ayurvedic pharmacy extracting fresh botanical oils, classical churnas, herbal decoctions, and bhasmas according to ancient Charaka Samhita formulas.

Detox & Rejuvenation

3. PANCHKARMA

Specialized 5-step deep purification treatments including Shirodhara, Sarvanga Abhyanga, Basti, Nasya, and herbal steam chambers on authentic wooden Droni therapy beds.

Sacred Cattle Sanctuary

4. GOW SHALA

Traditional indigenous sanctuary caring for sacred Gir and Desi cows. Generates fresh A2 milk, Vedic cultured ghee, Panchagavya, and offers meditative morning visits.

River Excursions

5. BOATING CLUB

Tranquil wooden boat excursions along the sacred Mandakini river. Private morning cruises to Ramghat for the Aarti ceremony and quiet contemplative forest riverbanks.

Artisanal Hearth

6. BAKERY

Artisanal in-house bakery baking wholesome whole-wheat loaves, jaggery tea biscuits, dry fruit rusks, and chemical-free baked goods fresh every dawn.
